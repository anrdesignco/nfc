SETUP GUIDE

Program your tag in six steps.

All you need is an NFC-compatible smartphone, the free NFC Tools app, and your NFC Paw Print tag.

01

Download NFC Tools

Install NFC Tools from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

02

Open the Write tab

Open the app, select Write, and then choose Add a record.

03

Choose what opens

Select a website, contact card, phone number, text message, or another supported record type.

04

Add your information

Enter the information you want someone to see when they scan your pet’s tag.

05

Write to the tag

Tap Write and hold the paw print against your phone until the success message appears.

06

Test before use

Close the app, unlock your phone, and scan the tag again to confirm the correct information opens.

BEST PRACTICE

For the cleanest experience, write one mobile-friendly link containing your pet’s contact and care information. Updating that page later is easier than rewriting several separate records.

NFC Paw Print Setup

HOW TO SCAN

Placement matters.

NFC antenna placement varies by phone. Move the tag slowly around the back of the device until the phone detects it.

  • iPhone Start near the top edge or upper back of the phone.
  • Android Start near the centre or upper back of the phone.
  • Before scanning Make sure the phone is unlocked.
  • If it does not scan Remove thick, magnetic, metallic, or wallet-style phone cases.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Most issues are easy to fix.

The phone does not detect the tag.

Make sure the phone supports NFC, unlock the screen, remove thick or metallic phone cases, and slowly move the tag around the back of the phone. On some Android devices, NFC must be enabled manually in Settings.

The tag will not write.

Confirm that you selected Write in NFC Tools. Wait for the app to prompt you before touching the tag to the phone, and hold the tag still until the success message appears.

The wrong information opens.

Return to NFC Tools, create the corrected record, and write it to the tag again. Always test the tag after making any changes.

Can I rewrite the tag?

Yes. The tag can be overwritten with new information unless it has been permanently locked through the app.

Does this replace my pet’s microchip?

No. The NFC tag is an additional identification aid. It does not replace a registered microchip, pet licence, or secure physical identification.

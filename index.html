NFC Paw Print Setup

Thank you for purchasing an A&R Design NFC Paw Print Pet Tag. Follow the instructions below to program, test and update your tag.

Your tag arrives blank. You control what information is written to it. A&R Design does not collect or store your pet’s personal information.

Before you begin

You will need:

  • An NFC-compatible iPhone or Android phone
  • The free NFC Tools app
  • Your A&R Design NFC Paw Print Tag

How to program your tag

1

Download NFC Tools

Install the NFC Tools app from your phone’s official app store and open it.

2

Select Write

In NFC Tools, open the Write section and select Add a record.

3

Choose what the tag opens

Select a website, contact, phone number, text message or another available record.

For the best lost-pet experience, we recommend linking to one mobile-friendly pet information page containing your preferred contact details.

4

Enter your information

Enter the information or website address and save the record.

5

Write the tag

Press Write. When prompted, hold the paw-print tag against your phone’s NFC scanning area until the app confirms that writing is complete.

6

Test it

Close the app, unlock your phone and scan the tag again. Confirm that the correct information opens before attaching the tag to your pet’s collar.

Where should I scan?

NFC antenna placement varies by phone. On many iPhones, scanning works best near the upper portion of the phone. On Android phones, the scanning area is commonly near the centre or upper back.

Move the tag slowly around the back of the phone if it is not detected immediately. Remove thick or metallic phone cases while programming or testing.

Updating the information

Open NFC Tools, create the updated record and write it to the tag again. Test the tag after every change.

Recommended information

  • Pet’s name
  • Owner’s first name
  • Primary phone number
  • Backup emergency contact
  • Relevant medical or care information
  • A short “Please contact my owner” message
Privacy reminder: Only include information you are comfortable sharing with anyone who scans the tag. Avoid passwords, payment details or other highly sensitive information.

Troubleshooting

The phone does not detect the tag:

  • Make sure the phone supports NFC.
  • Unlock the phone before scanning.
  • Remove a thick or metallic case.
  • Move the tag around the back of the phone slowly.
  • Confirm NFC is enabled in the phone settings.
  • Try programming or testing it with another phone.

The wrong information appears:

Return to NFC Tools, create the correct record and write the tag again.

Need help?

Send us a message and let us know which phone model you are using and what happens when you attempt to scan the tag.

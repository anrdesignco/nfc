SETUP GUIDE

Program your NFC product in six steps.

All you need is an NFC-compatible smartphone, the free NFC Tools app, and your A&R NFC product.

01

Download NFC Tools

Install NFC Tools from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

02

Open the Write tab

Open the app, select Write, and then choose Add a record.

03

Choose what opens

Select a website, contact card, phone number, text message, or another supported record type.

04

Add your information

Enter the information or link you want to open when someone scans your NFC product.

05

Write to the product

Tap Write and hold your NFC product against your phone until the success message appears.

06

Test before use

Close the app, unlock your phone, and scan the product again to confirm the correct information opens.

BEST PRACTICE

For the cleanest experience, write one mobile-friendly link that leads to everything you want to share. Updating that page later is easier than rewriting several separate records.

A&R NFC Setup

HOW TO SCAN

Placement matters.

NFC antenna placement varies by phone. Move the product slowly around the back of the device until the phone detects it.

  • iPhone Start near the top edge or upper back of the phone.
  • Android Start near the centre or upper back of the phone.
  • Before scanning Make sure the phone is unlocked.
  • If it does not scan Remove thick, magnetic, metallic, or wallet-style phone cases.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Most issues are easy to fix.

The phone does not detect the NFC product.

Make sure the phone supports NFC, unlock the screen, remove thick or metallic phone cases, and slowly move the product around the back of the phone. On some Android devices, NFC must be enabled manually in Settings.

The NFC product will not write.

Confirm that you selected Write in NFC Tools. Wait for the app to prompt you before touching the product to the phone, and hold it still until the success message appears.

The wrong information opens.

Return to NFC Tools, create the corrected record, and write it to the NFC product again. Always test it after making any changes.

Can I rewrite the NFC product?

Yes. The NFC chip can be overwritten with new information unless it has been permanently locked through the app.

Can one NFC product open multiple links?

Yes. The easiest method is to program one link that opens a mobile-friendly landing page containing all of the links, contact details, or actions you want to share.
